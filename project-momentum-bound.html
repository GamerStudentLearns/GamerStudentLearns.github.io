Momentum Bound

Overview

Partway through my first year, some of the people on my course decided our games would have interwoven lore. Momentum Bound was made as part of that, and it still receives updates to this day.

For a while, I had wanted to make a roguelike, because I always found 'The Binding Of Isaac' to be an extremely fun game and I wanted to be able to look at a game like it and think 'I did that'. The perfect opportunity arose when our course gave us a 2D project that we had to do, so I took that in stride and began to work on Momentum bound. Through our projects, me and my coursemates coincidentally made a character named 'Craig' so we decided that it would be the same man and Momentum Bound wraps all the lore of Phase 1 into one place. I still work on the game till now.

Design & lore

It's a roguelike inspired by The Binding of Isaac, and it brings together most of the lore of 'Craig' up to the point this game released.

In wanting to tie the lore in with all my coursemates, I used a lot of designs from all our games, for example the human design of Craig from Team Chiburren's 'Everdew'. I also added lots of items as references to pop culture that I like and also more things from my coursemates games. I came up with ideas to make sneaky references. I included characters from everdew like Butters, Margarine and Craig. I added these people as bosses, that you'll have to play the game to experience. I also added a case report of sorts that lets the player know most of the story without actually having to play through the other games.

Where it's headed

If I keep going, I'll add more lore, more bosses, and a lot more polish to make sure the game looks and plays well.

My most recent fix was reparing controller support that broke and fixing the lore pages DLC that had broken from a previous update. This had taken a while to fix as I had been busy with other things. I think for future updates, i'll take a leaf out of Edmund McMillen's book, and release big DLC's adding new floors, bosses, enemies and items. I'll keep working on it for a while because it's such a fun game to work on and I enjoy having something in the background to fall back on and work on if I get bored or need something familiar to experience.
