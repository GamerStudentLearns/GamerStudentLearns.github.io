Who I am

I've known from a young age that I wanted to design video games. What made it click was playing Final Fantasy XV — the environments, the combat, even the car you drive between them. I wanted to be able to look at a game like that and think, "I made that."

For the past year and a bit I've been building on my game design skills at New College, on projects that have stretched what I know and pushed me toward my goal of one day working somewhere like Square Enix.
